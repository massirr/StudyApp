# Vehicle checks — Belgian category-B practical exam (Flanders)

Source notes the `vehicle-check` subject is authored from. Researched 2026-11-04. Where a source is third-party, it is said so. Exam centre: AIBV Asse-Mollem (Flemish Region, GOCA Vlaanderen rules).

## 1. What the exam is

The practical exam has two parts that are both taken at the same centre: the **GOCA Risk Perception Test** (computer, 5 test films) and the **proef op de openbare weg** (road test). The road test has three parts: **voorafgaande controles**, **manoeuvres in beweging** and **zelfstandig rijden** (10-15 min, GPS plus road signs).
Source: GOCA Vlaanderen, DOC. 133 "Cat. B - Praktijkexamen" — https://autoveiligheid.be/downloaden/cat-b

A sworn interpreter (French, German or English) may come along on the road test; the interpreter may not be the accompanying person. Same source.

## 2. Voorafgaande controles (official breakdown)

From GOCA DOC. 133:

1. **Voorzorgen** (precautions): the examiner asks you to switch off the engine, get out, get back in and lock the car. This usually happens during a parking manoeuvre, otherwise at another moment.
2. **Instellingen** (settings): you explain how you take a correct seating position and set the backrest, headrest and mirrors, and you fasten the seatbelt.
3. **Controles**:
   - **Systematisch** (always, one by one, you operate each): ontijsing/ontwaseming voor en achter (front and rear demisting), verlichting (dimlicht, grootlicht, mistlicht), toeter (horn), richtingaanwijzers (indicators), remlichten (brake lights).
   - **Steekproefsgewijs** (one item, chosen by computer): slijtage van de banden (one front wheel), bandenspanning, motorolie, remvloeistof, koelvloeistof, ruitenwisservloeistof (washer fluid). Time: **2 min 30 s** for checks that need the bonnet open, **1 min 30 s** for the others.

The Belgisch Staatsblad exam text (KB amending the 1998 exam rules; Moniteur belge 22.02.2017, annex 5 III B point 17) lists the same items: set the seat for a correct posture; set the mirrors, seatbelt and headrest; check that the doors are closed; spot-check tyres, brakes, steering, fluids, lights, ventilation, indicators and horn; take the necessary precautions when leaving the vehicle.
Source: https://codex.vlaanderen.be/PrintDocument.ashx?id=1025928 and https://code-de-la-route.be/fr/regulation/amendment/download/tmt4bdtyrz

Note: wipers are not in the official list. Washer **fluid** is. Wipers are covered in the subject only as extra vocabulary.

## 3. Lighting rules (Belgium, not the Netherlands)

- **Mistachterlicht** (rear fog light): when fog or snow reduces visibility to **less than 100 m**. (The Netherlands uses 50 m.) Switch it off in queues because it dazzles the driver behind.
- **Mistlichten voor** (front fog lights): never mandatory; allowed in fog, snowfall or heavy rain.
- **Dimlicht** (dipped beam) in daytime: required when visibility is below **200 m** (rain, hail, snow) and in tunnels.
- **Dagrijlichten** (daytime running lights): front only; they do not replace dipped beam because the rear lights stay off.
Sources: VRT NWS https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2021/12/28/dagrijlichten-en-dimlichten-op-je-auto-wat-is-het-verschil-en-h/ ; AutoScout24.be https://www.autoscout24.be/nl/informeren/advies/kosten-en-wetgeving/wanneer-mistlichten-aan/ ; ANWB (NL/BE comparison) https://www.anwb.nl/auto/onderhoud-en-reparatie/verlichting/mistlampen
Not tested because sources disagree: the heavy-rain nuance for the rear fog light, and a minimum visibility for front fog lights.

## 4. Fluids and tyres (procedures)

- **Oil (motorolie)**: level ground, engine off. Cold engine is best; after driving wait at least 10 minutes. Pull the dipstick (oliepeilstok), wipe it, push it fully back in, pull it out again and read the film between MIN and MAX. Ideal is about halfway. Too much oil is also harmful. Sources: Kwik-Fit https://www.kwik-fit.nl/kennisbank/gidsen/hoe-controleer-ik-het-oliepeil-van-mijn-auto ; ANWB https://www.anwb.nl/auto/onderhoud-en-reparatie/onderhoud-doe-het-zelf/oliepeil-controleren
- **Coolant (koelvloeistof)**: check on a cold engine; read the level on the see-through expansion reservoir between MIN and MAX; never open the cap on a hot engine (liquid can spray out and burn). Sources: ANWB https://www.anwb.nl/auto/onderhoud-en-reparatie/vloeistoffen/koelvloeistof-bijvullen and https://www.anwb.nl/auto/onderhoud-en-reparatie/auto-onderdelen/koelvloeistof
- **Brake fluid (remvloeistof)**: level between MIN and MAX on the reservoir; the level falls slowly as brake pads wear, which is normal; a sudden or large drop needs a workshop. Source: Kia owner's manual (NL) https://ownersmanual.kia.com/docview/webhelp/doc/51376c09-deee-4dbe-b8c6-d4408a11ce66/topics/chapter8_9_1.html
- **Washer fluid (ruitenwisservloeistof / ruitensproeiervloeistof)**: reservoir cap usually has a windscreen-with-spray symbol. Source: Kwik-Fit https://www.kwik-fit.nl/kennisbank/gidsen/hoe-vul-ik-mijn-ruitensproeiervloeistof-bij
- **Tyre pressure (bandenspanning)**: check with cold tyres; the correct value is in the owner's manual and on a sticker in the door frame or inside the fuel flap; do not let air out of warm tyres. Sources: ANWB https://www.anwb.nl/auto/onderhoud-en-reparatie/onderhoud-doe-het-zelf/banden ; AutoRAI https://autorai.nl/bandenspanning-alles-wat-je-moet-weten/
- **Tyre wear (bandenslijtage)**: legal minimum tread depth in Belgium is **1.6 mm**; the **TWI** marks on the sidewall point to the wear indicators, small rubber blocks in the grooves; when they are level with the tread the tyre is worn out. Source: Test-Aankoop https://www.test-aankoop.be/mobiliteit/banden/antwoord-van-expert/bandengrip-verkeersveiligheid

## 5. Dashboard warning lights (bonus, not in the official list)

Colour code: red = act now (stop safely), orange/yellow = have it checked soon, green = a system is working, blue = information (e.g. main beam). Lights come on briefly at ignition as a bulb check. Red oil-pressure and red battery lights mean stop.
Sources: AutoScout24.be https://www.autoscout24.be/nl/informeren/advies/autotechnologie/wat-betekenen-de-lichtjes-op-uw-dashboard/ ; ANWB https://www.anwb.nl/auto/onderhoud-en-reparatie/autopech/waarschuwingslampjes
