## Marker numbers on the engine-bay photo

They use the numbers of the former schematic diagram (`engine-bay.svg`, now removed because no question uses it):

1. Oliepeilstok / oil dipstick (yellow loop)
2. Olievuldop / oil filler cap (yellow)
3. Koelvloeistofreservoir / coolant reservoir (pressure cap with warning symbol)
4. Remvloeistofreservoir / brake fluid reservoir
6. Accu / battery

Number 5 (washer fluid) has no marker in this photo: the washer cap is outside the frame. Its question uses a second photo, `washer-cap-photo.jpg`, which has only marker 5 on the blue washer fluid cap (with the windscreen-washer symbol). That photo shows an electric Opel Corsa-e, so it has no dipstick or oil filler cap; use it only to learn what the washer cap looks like.

## Not replaced (and why)

- **Front and rear lights:** none of the free photos had the lights on. With every lamp off you can't tell them apart, and the layout differs per car. The diagrams stay.
- **Dashboard and warning symbols:** the symbols are standard, and a diagram reads more clearly than a photo of one dashboard.
- **Dipstick A/B/C levels, tread cross-section:** these compare levels side by side, which a single photo can't show.

These photos deliberately show a different car from the exam car. The learner takes the exam in their own car (M36 provisional licence) and wants to find the parts on it themselves, so the app doesn't include photos of that car. Use these photos to learn what each part looks like, then find it on your own car.
