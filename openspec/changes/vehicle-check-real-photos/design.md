## Context

The current diagrams live in `public/images/vehicle-check/*.svg`, generated by a script that is NOT in the repo (marker coordinates were hand-tuned). Questions reference them via `question.image {src, alt}` (alt is mandatory; enforced by `validateSubject`). The numbered-marker questions ("Welk nummer is...?") rely on the numbers in the picture.

## Decisions

1. **Own photos first.** Photos of the driving school's car beat any stock photo because the layout under the bonnet differs per car. Fallback: Wikimedia Commons files with a free licence (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain).
2. **Numbers are drawn onto the photo**, not a separate overlay file: export one flat JPEG/WebP per photo with numbered circles. Keep a labelled NL/EN copy outside the app for the learner's phone gallery.
3. **Credits.** The schema has no credit field. Put the credit in the question `explanation` ("Foto: <author>, <licence>, <url>") and add the source to `sources` with a `usageNote`. Do not extend the schema in this change.
4. **Keep the SVGs as fallback** for any topic without a photo; delete an SVG only when no question references it.
5. **No hotlinking.** Copy files into `public/images/vehicle-check/` so the app works offline and does not break if a host changes a file.
6. **Size.** Resize to at most 1200 px wide, quality 80, target under 250 KB each.

## Risks

- Real photos are busier than diagrams: use large markers with a white outline and check legibility at 400 px wide.
- Licence mistakes: verify licence and author on the file page before use; never use "all rights reserved" or unknown-licence images.
- If the real car differs from the diagram, answers such as "number 3 = coolant reservoir" change; update the explanation, and keep the correct-answer letters balanced (answerDistribution test).
